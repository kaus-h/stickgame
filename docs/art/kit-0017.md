# Kit 0017 mechanical slicing

Kit 0017 provides the in-scope terrain stamps and three background strips from the approved sheets. The original source PNGs remain in `assets/art/kit-0017/source/`; runtime files are the compact 1x outputs in `assets/art/kit-0017/1x/`. The authoring `manifest.json` records exact source crops, source scale, output dimensions, and semantics. `runtime.json` contains only terrain entries T01–T23 and T25–T28, without source paths or crop data; pool/background assets load by direct asset URLs. Compact runtime keys `role`, `semantic`, and `datum` map to authoring `renderRole`, `semanticRole`, and `contactDatum1x`. Rebuild both manifests and the crops with `node scripts/art/slice-kit-0017.mjs`.

Terrain artwork is rendered at 64 px/m from 144 source px/m. Pool and terrain stamps retain their 1x output size; background strips render to 1024 px width. White paper is removed into alpha, paper/ink levels are quantized for compact PNGs, and the red waterline remains red only on T34–T39. The renderer applies the lighter terrain treatment; source pixels remain unchanged.

## Sheet B mapping

T34 is the surface span, T35–T36 meet the left/right bank walls, T37–T38 are the inner-left/inner-right pool corners, and T39 is the depth fill. The source meniscus and joins sit over a dense procedural red-pen body filling the entire hazard polygon (doc 09 H4, locked after owner playtest). T39's pale glyphs are not drawn. Pool art is a hazard visual, not a landing contour.

T40–T42 are the distant, middle, and near-background strips in sheet order. They are 1024 px wide and carry no collision/contact guides.

## Datum metadata

`contactDatum1x` points/segments use each output PNG's top-left-origin pixel space. The manifest records top surfaces for landing assets, bottom edges for T04/T18/T21, vertical guides for T05/T06/T16, and paired segments for corners and wall brackets. T01, T07, pool art, and backgrounds have no solid-contact datum. `hazardDatum1x` marks the provisional pool meniscus. These guides encode the source crop at output scale and are calibration references; collision remains authored separately from the ink.

Each original sheet and every generated PNG has a provenance row with its prompt ID and generation date.

## Runtime encoding

`scripts/art/encode-kit-runtime.py` (Pillow with WebP support) produces runtime WebPs while retaining every canonical PNG. All 36 kit 0017 copies preserve exact alpha everywhere and RGB on every visible pixel. The two 0016 compatibility textures use bounded RGB palette rounding with exact dimensions and alpha: ledge step 32, pool step 16. The encoder verifies maximum composited channel differences of 4 and 8 respectively over notebook paper; contact shapes and guides are unchanged. This recovers bundle space after integrating main without changing budgets or quantizing the approved 0017 art.

Terrain classification ignores spike overlays when choosing the supporting crest, so teeth cannot cut a pale notch out of their floor. Actual pool boundaries and solid contacts still suppress internal contours.
