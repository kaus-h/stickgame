# 0012: base world look v3, reference handwriting in a light doodle world (GMR-105)

**Tool:** ChatGPT image generation, GPT-6 at High effort, 2026-10-09. Made in the StickGame project chat "Apply StickGame Art Style", where the owner uploaded their favourite reference pieces themselves and directed that game assets be generated there (07A Part 2D, as amended 2026-10-08). The artist was not named in any prompt. Result: `assets/concepts/0012-world-ref-light.png` (1672×941).

**History:** the first image in that chat used castles, crows, a skull rock, thorns, roses and fangs. The owner judged it "wayy too goth core" and it was discarded. This redo keeps only the references' _drawing handwriting_.

Prompt (verbatim, follow-up in the same chat):

> Way too goth. Redo it much lighter. Keep the SAME layout idea but change the mood: this is a playful, slightly odd doodle world in a student's notebook, not a gothic one. REMOVE: castles, cathedrals, bridges, crows, skull rocks, roses, thorny vines, barbed wire, fangs, dungeon doors, dark dramatic sky masses. Keep the page mostly bright paper-white. USE FROM THE REFERENCES ONLY THE DRAWING HANDWRITING: wobbly confident marker contours with strong weight contrast (fat outer lines, hairline scratchy inner hatching), a few SMALL solid-black accents (under-sides of ledges, the hero's boots and eye sockets, a doorway), scratchy crosshatching and stipple used lightly, imperfect overshooting lines. WORLD CONTENT instead: chunky doodled ground blocks and floating cliff chunks with scribbly grass tufts; a couple of torn notebook-paper scraps taped on as thin ledges with a dark drawn top edge; faint blue ballpoint background doodles (rolling hills, a little cloud, a small sun, stars, an arrow, a wonky little tree); a small doodled hut with an open dark doorway as the exit on the right. Gameplay moment unchanged: stick-figure hero (round lumpy head, solid black eye sockets with no tears, cross-stitched mouth, single-line limbs, black boots, no scarf) swinging right on a dashed-line rope from a round anchor ring; red ink pool with a few short red-tipped spikes below. Red only for deadly things. No text, no UI.

**Originality check (07A Part 2B):** passes. There are no red-tear skulls, no wordmark or lettering, and no copied character or composition. The hero keeps our own design.

**Review:**

- **Works:**
  - Bold wobbly contours with hairline hatching on the cliffs.
  - Floating-island chunks with crisp grassy top edges.
  - Taped notebook-paper ledges with a dark top edge.
  - Blue ballpoint background (sun, cloud, hills, stars, arrow, tree, flowers).
  - The hut works as the exit.
  - Red appears only on the hazards.
- **Watch:**
  - The rope reads more like a chain than a dashed line in places. The in-game rope stays dashed (locked canon).
  - Blue flowers and plants on ledge tops are fine as decoration, but must sit behind the collision edge.
