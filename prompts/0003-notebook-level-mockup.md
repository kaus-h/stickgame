## Prompt

New direction from the owner: that is too goth-core. The entire game must feel like it was drawn on paper, in every aspect. Generate a 16:9 landscape mockup of an in-game screenshot where the whole level is drawn in pen on a sheet of college-ruled notebook paper: slightly warm off-white paper with subtle fiber texture, thin light-blue horizontal ruled lines, a pink-red vertical margin line, three punched binder holes along the left edge, and wire spiral binding visible at the left edge of the page. Everything in the level is a pen doodle drawn on that page by a bored, talented student during class: black fineliner contours that are slightly wobbly, blue ballpoint crosshatching and scribble shading for mid-tones, a few small solid-black filled areas only, visible pen pressure variation, ink that sometimes overlaps the ruled lines. Level contents in strict side view: stone-block platforms drawn as pen boxes with crosshatched sides and a clean bold top edge; a small doodled hero in side profile (a stick-limbed figure with a round skull-like head and hollow eyes, no tears) swinging on a rope drawn as a dashed pen line from a doodled iron-ring anchor; the active anchor marked with a quick yellow highlighter swipe; a row of doodled thorn and tooth spikes along the bottom of the page shaded in red ballpoint; a small doodled bat with crosshatched wings; the level exit drawn as a little arched door sketched into the page. Interface drawn on the paper too: a handwritten timer in the top corner, a death counter as tally marks written in the margin, and a small yellow sticky note with a scribbled hint. Mood: charming, melancholic punk doodle on homework paper, not dark, not gothic, mostly white paper with ink lines. No real text except the timer digits and tally marks. Do not imitate any specific existing artist or artwork.
